# 03 — Complete Candlestick Guide for Price Action Trading

## 1. Core Principle: Context Beats Pattern

A candlestick pattern in the middle of a random sideways range has **near-zero edge**.
A candlestick pattern only becomes high-probability when it forms at a **key location**:
1. Major Support or Resistance zone (PDH, PDL, swing level, psychological round number)
2. Dynamic S/R (VWAP, 20 EMA, 50 EMA, trendline)
3. After an extended impulse move or liquidity sweep (stop hunt)
4. Aligned with the higher-timeframe trend (15m / 1H / Daily)

---

## 2. Candlestick Anatomy (What Each Part Tells You)

- **Open → Close (Body):** Shows who won the candle.
  - Large bullish/bearish body = strong conviction & institutional participation.
  - Shrinking bodies during a move = momentum deceleration / exhaustion.
- **Upper Wick (Shadow):** Buyers tried to push higher, sellers rejected them back down (supply / selling pressure).
- **Lower Wick (Shadow):** Sellers tried to push lower, buyers absorbed supply and drove price back up (demand / buying pressure).
- **Close Position (Crucial):** Where the candle closes inside its total High–Low range matters most. A close in the top 25% of the candle signals buyer control; a close in the bottom 25% signals seller control.

## 3. Single-Candle Patterns

| Pattern | Visual Structure | Location Required | Signal & Confirmation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Hammer (Bullish Pin Bar)** | Small body at top; lower wick ≥ 2× body; little/no upper wick | At Support / pullback low / sweep of PDL | Bullish reversal. Enter on break of Hammer high (or slight retest); SL below Hammer low. |
| **Shooting Star (Bearish Pin Bar)** | Small body at bottom; upper wick ≥ 2× body; little/no lower wick | At Resistance / rally high / sweep of PDH | Bearish reversal. Enter on break of Shooting Star low; SL above Shooting Star high. |
| **Inverted Hammer** | Small body at bottom, long upper wick | At Support after downtrend | Buyers tested upside; **must wait** for next candle to close bullish above its high. |
| **Hanging Man** | Hammer shape, but at the top of an uptrend | At Resistance | Warning of hidden selling; confirm only if next candle closes below its low. |
| **Bullish / Bearish Marubozu** | Large full body, tiny or zero wicks | Breakout of S/R, ORB, or consolidation | Strong momentum continuation. Watch for shallow pullback (30–50% of body) rather than chasing blindly if extended. |
| **Doji (Standard / Long-Legged)** | Open ≈ Close | At extreme S/R | Pure equilibrium/indecision. Never trade alone — wait for next candle to break high or low. |
| **Dragonfly Doji** | Open ≈ Close ≈ High; very long lower wick | At Support | Strong buyer rejection of lower prices (bullish). |
| **Gravestone Doji** | Open ≈ Close ≈ Low; very long upper wick | At Resistance | Strong seller rejection of higher prices (bearish). |
| **Spinning Top** | Small body centered between roughly equal wicks | After a strong trend | Momentum pause; signals consolidation or reversal if confirmed. |

## 4. Two-Candle Patterns

| Pattern | Structure | Meaning & Trade Trigger |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Bullish Engulfing** | Small red candle followed by a large green candle whose body completely engulfs the prior red body | Buyers overwhelmed sellers. Best at Support / VWAP pullback. SL below pattern low. |
| **Bearish Engulfing** | Small green candle followed by a large red candle whose body completely engulfs the prior green body | Sellers overwhelmed buyers. Best at Resistance / supply zone. SL above pattern high. |
| **Inside Bar (Harami)** | Candle 2's High is lower than Candle 1's High AND Low is higher than Candle 1's Low | Volatility compression / pause before expansion. Trade the breakout of the mother bar (or inside bar) in trend direction. |
| **Fakeout Inside Bar (Fakey)** | Inside bar breaks one side briefly, then reverses and closes beyond the opposite side | Classic institutional trap — high-momentum move in the reversal direction. |
| **Piercing Line (Bullish)** | Red candle followed by green candle opening lower and closing > 50% into the red body | Strong demand response at support; confirm above green high. |
| **Dark Cloud Cover (Bearish)** | Green candle followed by red candle opening higher and closing > 50% into the green body | Strong supply response at resistance; confirm below red low. |
| **Tweezer Bottom / Top** | Two consecutive candles testing and rejecting the exact same low (bottom) or high (top) | Double rejection confirming an institutional defense level. |
| **Railroad Tracks** | Two opposite-colored candles of nearly identical full-body size side by side | Sudden violent shift in control; strong reversal signal at S/R. |

## 5. Three-Candle Patterns

| Pattern | Structure | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Morning Star** | Large red candle → small indecision candle (Doji/Spinning Top) → strong green candle closing well into candle 1 | High-reliability bullish reversal at major support. |
| **Evening Star** | Large green candle → small indecision candle → strong red candle closing well into candle 1 | High-reliability bearish reversal at major resistance. |
| **Three White Soldiers / Three Black Crows** | Three consecutive strong-bodied candles closing near their highs/lows with small wicks | Institutional trend ignition — look to join on the first shallow pullback rather than chasing candle 3. |
| **Rising / Falling Three Methods** | Strong trend candle → 2–3 small counter-trend candles contained inside → strong continuation candle breaking out | Textbook trend continuation (flag in candlestick form). |

## 6. Intraday NIFTY & BANKNIFTY Candlestick Rules

1. **Wait for Candle Close:** An unclosed 5-minute candle can look like a huge Marubozu at minute 3 and finish as a Shooting Star fakeout by minute 5. Always let the trigger candle **close** (especially on 5m / 15m).
2. **Avoid the First 5-Minute Candle Noise:** The 09:15–09:20 candle digests overnight global cues and market-order clusters. Use it to mark the opening range, not as a blind reversal signal.
3. **Read the Spot Chart, Not Just the Option Chart:** Option candles get distorted by theta decay and IV spikes. Identify candlestick setups on **NIFTY / BANKNIFTY Spot (or Futures)**, then verify the option premium isn't facing an obvious resistance wall.
4. **Combine Multiple Candles Mentally (Blending):** A red candle followed immediately by a green bullish engulfing candle on the 5m chart *is* a single **Hammer** on the 10m chart. Understanding candle blending helps you see true rejection across timeframes.
5. **Size Filter (Abnormal Candles):** If a single 5m NIFTY candle is huge (e.g., 70–100+ points on news), your stop-loss becomes too wide and reward-to-risk breaks down — skip it or wait for a pullback/retest.
