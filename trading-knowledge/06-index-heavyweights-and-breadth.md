# 06 — Index Heavyweights, Sector Breadth & Global Cues

## 1. Why Tracking Heavyweights Prevents Fake Index Trades

NIFTY 50 and BANKNIFTY are **free-float market-capitalization weighted indices**. A handful of heavyweight stocks drive more than half of every intraday candle.

- If NIFTY breaks above resistance **only** because of a single stock spike while HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, and Reliance are red/stalling → **high fakeout risk**.
- If **3–4 top heavyweights** break out simultaneously in the same direction → **high-probability institutional trend move**.

## 3. BANKNIFTY Heavyweights

Under SEBI/NSE's stricter non-benchmark index concentration framework, single-stock weights in BANKNIFTY are capped more tightly than in earlier years, though the top 5 banks still dominate intraday movement:

| Constituent | Role in BANKNIFTY |
| --- | --- |
| **HDFC Bank** (~18%) | Primary heavyweight; rarely trends cleanly without HDFC Bank participation |
| **ICICI Bank** (~15%) | Institutional momentum leader |
| **State Bank of India (SBI)** | High-beta PSU bank catalyst; sharp breakouts |
| **Kotak Mahindra Bank** (~11%) | Often lags or leads rotation moves |
| **Axis Bank** (~10%) | High intraday volatility; tracks private bank flows |

- **Rule:** Never buy a BANKNIFTY CE breakout if **both** HDFC Bank and ICICI Bank are trading below their VWAP and making lower highs.

## 4. Market Breadth & Divergence Filters

1. **NIFTY 50 Advance–Decline Ratio:**
   - **> 38 Advancing / < 12 Declining** → Broad bullish trend day (buy dips on CE).
   - **< 12 Advancing / > 38 Declining** → Broad bearish trend day (sell rallies / buy PE).
   - **22–28 split** → Sector rotation / chop (reduce size or avoid option buying).
2. **NIFTY vs. BANKNIFTY Divergence:**
   - When NIFTY makes a new intraday high but BANKNIFTY fails to break its prior high (bearish divergence), momentum is fragile.
   - Best directional option trades happen when **NIFTY and BANKNIFTY break structure together**.
3. **India VIX Divergence:**
   - Market falling + VIX spiking sharply = panic selling (PE premiums expand fast via both Delta and Vega).
   - Market rising slowly + VIX falling = steady grind up (CE gains via Delta but faces mild Vega drag; spreads work great).

## 5. Pre-Market & Global Cues Routine (08:30 – 09:10 IST)

1. **GIFT NIFTY (NSE IX — trades ~21 hrs/day across two sessions: morning through 15:40 IST and 16:05–02:45 IST):**
   - Compare **GIFT NIFTY** to **NSE NIFTY Near-Month Futures** (NOT Spot!) to estimate the real opening gap.
   - *Gap Classification:*
     - **Flat Open (±0.25%):** Respect prior day structure (PDH/PDL/trend continuation).
     - **Moderate Gap (0.3% – 0.8%):** Watch for gap-fill attempt or 15m ORB continuation.
     - **Large Gap (> 1.0%):** Overnight traders book profits at the open — **never chase the first 5m candle**; wait for 15–30 minutes of structure to form.
2. **Overnight US Markets:** S&P 500, Nasdaq 100 (strong cue for Indian IT — `INFY`, `TCS`), US 10-Year Treasury Yield, and Dollar Index (**DXY**).
3. **Key Macro Cross-Assets for India:**
   - **Brent Crude Oil:** Sharp crude spikes pressure Indian inflation/OMC stocks and broad sentiment.
   - **USD/INR:** Rapid rupee weakening often coincides with FII equity outflows (pressuring Banking/NIFTY) while occasionally lifting IT exporters.
4. **NSE Pre-Open Equilibrium (09:07 – 09:08 IST):** Locks exact NIFTY 50 opening print — immediately note whether it opens **above PDH**, **below PDL**, or **inside previous day's range**.
