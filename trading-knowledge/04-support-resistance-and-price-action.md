# 04 — Support, Resistance & Price Action Mastery

## 1. Support & Resistance (S/R) Fundamentals

Support and Resistance are **zones (bands)**, never razor-thin single prices, because institutional orders are staged across price ranges.

### Hierarchy of Key Levels (Strongest to Tactical)
1. **Higher-Timeframe Horizontal Swings (Daily / Weekly / 1H):** Major turning points where large volume reversed price.
2. **Previous Day Levels (Must Mark Every Morning Before 09:15):**
   - **PDH** (Previous Day High)
   - **PDL** (Previous Day Low)
   - **PDC** (Previous Day Close — often acts as a gap-fill magnet)
3. **Psychological Round Numbers + Option Chain OI Strikes:**
   - **NIFTY:** Every 100 and 500 points (e.g., `24,800`, `25,000`, `25,500`)
   - **BANKNIFTY:** Every 500 and 1,000 points (e.g., `53,000`, `53,500`, `54,000`)
   - *These levels become ultra-strong when aligned with maximum Call/Put OI.*
4. **Intraday Anchors:**
   - **ORB (Opening Range Breakout):** First 5-min or 15-min High and Low.
   - **Today's High / Today's Low**
   - **CPR (Central Pivot Range):** Pivot, TC (Top Central), BC (Bottom Central) — a **narrow CPR** signals a potential trending day; a **wide CPR** signals a range-bound day.
5. **Dynamic Support & Resistance:**
   - **VWAP (Volume Weighted Average Price):** Primary institutional intraday benchmark. Above VWAP = bullish bias; below VWAP = bearish bias. (Use Futures VWAP for NIFTY/BANKNIFTY since spot index has no direct volume.)
   - **EMAs:** `9 EMA` (strong momentum trend), `20 EMA` (healthy intraday pullback mean), `50 EMA` & `200 EMA` (major trend filters).
   - **Trendlines & Channels:** Validated only after **at least 2 clean swing points** (3rd touch is tradeable).

### Critical S/R Principles
- **Polarity Flip (Role Reversal):** Broken Resistance turns into Support; broken Support turns into Resistance. The **Breakout + Retest** of a flipped level is one of the highest-R:R entries in trading.
- **Touch Count / Zone Fatigue:**
  - 1st & 2nd touch → Highest odds of a bounce/reversal.
  - 3rd & 4th touch → Liquidity gets absorbed each time; breakout probability increases sharply.
- **Supply & Demand Zones:** Look for the origin of a sharp, impulsive departure:
  - **Demand Zone (Buyers):** Drop-Base-Rally (DBR) or Rally-Base-Rally (RBR).
  - **Supply Zone (Sellers):** Rally-Base-Drop (RBD) or Drop-Base-Drop (DBD).
  - *Fresh (untested) zones work far better than repeatedly tested ones.*
- **Confluence Rule:** Never rely on one weak line. Stack 2–3 factors (e.g., **PDH flipped to Support + VWAP + 25,000 high Put OI + 5m Bullish Engulfing**).

## 2. Market Structure (The Backbone of Price Action)

Before looking for any trade, classify the current market structure:

### Three Market States
1. **Uptrend:** Series of **Higher Highs (HH)** and **Higher Lows (HL)**.
   - *Strategy:* Buy CE on pullbacks into HL / Support / VWAP / 20 EMA, or on confirmed breakout above HH. Never blindly short a strong uptrend.
2. **Downtrend:** Series of **Lower Highs (LH)** and **Lower Lows (LL)**.
   - *Strategy:* Buy PE on pullbacks into LH / Resistance / VWAP / 20 EMA, or on breakdown below LL.
3. **Sideways / Consolidation Range:** Equal highs and equal lows.
   - *Strategy:* **Danger zone for option buyers** (theta eats both CE and PE). Either sit out, trade only sharp rejections at range extremes, or deploy hedged option-selling strategies.

### BOS vs. CHoCH (Structure Shifts)
- **BOS (Break of Structure — Continuation):** In an uptrend, a candle **closes** above the previous swing high (HH). Confirms trend continuation.
- **CHoCH (Change of Character — Early Reversal Warning):** In an uptrend, price breaks and closes **below the most recent Higher Low (HL)**. Stop buying calls; look for a Lower High (LH) to enter puts.

## 3. Liquidity Sweeps, Stop Hunts & Fakeouts

Indian index options markets are famous for **stop-hunting** retail traders right around obvious levels (PDH, PDL, day high/low, equal highs/lows).

### How a Classic Liquidity Sweep Works (Bull Trap Example)
1. NIFTY builds clean resistance at `25,000` (equal highs). Retail breakout traders place buy orders at `25,005`; shorts place stop-losses at `25,010`.
2. Price spikes rapidly to `25,025`, triggering all breakout buys and short stop-losses (creating massive buy-side liquidity).
3. Institutions sell into that liquidity, pushing price right back **below `25,000`** before the 5m candle closes — leaving a long upper wick (Shooting Star / Sweep candle).
4. Trapped breakout buyers panic-exit, fueling a sharp drop toward VWAP or PDL.

**How to Trade It:** Enter PE when the next candle breaks below the sweep candle's low (or on a weak retest of `25,000`), with a tight SL just above the sweep wick high (`25,030`).

## 4. Breakout vs. Fakeout Checklist

Before buying an option on a breakout, check these **5 conditions**:

1. **Buildup Before Breakout (Best Filter):** Did price consolidate tightly right under resistance (forming higher lows / shrinking candles) before breaking out? Breakouts *after buildup* succeed far more often than vertical rallies slamming straight into resistance.
2. **Decisive Candle Close:** Wait for a strong candle **close** beyond the zone (at least 60–70% of the body outside the level), not just a wick poke.
3. **Volume / Momentum Expansion:** Futures volume spikes on the breakout candle (or option premium surges decisively).
4. **OI Unwinding Confirmation:** For an upside breakout, Call OI at the broken strike should stop rising or start unwinding while Put OI adds rapidly.
5. **Retest Behavior:** If you miss the initial candle, wait for price to pull back gently (small-bodied candles) to the broken level and print a rejection wick (Hammer / Engulfing) — then enter with a much tighter SL.

## 5. High-Probability Chart Patterns

### Reversal Patterns (Trade Only After Extended Moves at Major S/R)
- **Double Bottom ("W") / Double Top ("M"):** Strongest when the 2nd leg sweeps slightly past the 1st leg and snaps back (liquidity grab + divergence), confirmed by a **neckline break**.
- **Head & Shoulders / Inverse Head & Shoulders:** Signals exhaustion of trend structure; neckline break triggers target ≈ height of head-to-neckline.

### Continuation Patterns (Best for Intraday Option Buying)
- **Bull Flag / Bear Flag:** Sharp impulse pole followed by a tight, orderly counter-trend channel with declining volume → enter on flag breakout in the pole's direction.
- **Ascending Triangle (Bullish) / Descending Triangle (Bearish):** Flat resistance with rising Higher Lows (buyers stepping up aggressively) → high probability upside breakout.
- **Consolidation Box / NR7 Breakout:** Tight horizontal range during midday; explosive move once broken with volume.

## 6. Multi-Timeframe Analysis (MTFA) Workflow

Always analyze **top-down** so you don't buy a 5-minute breakout directly into a Daily resistance wall:

| Timeframe | Role | What to Mark |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Daily** | Macro Bias & Major Walls | Trend direction, PDH, PDL, PDC, gap zones, weekly highs/lows |
| **1 Hour / 15 Min** | Intraday Structure & Zones | Clean swing highs/lows, Supply/Demand zones, trendlines, BOS/CHoCH |
| **5 Min (Primary Trigger)** | Entry & Stop-Loss Execution | Candlesticks (Engulfing, Pin Bar, Inside Bar), VWAP pullbacks, Breakout + Retest |
| **1 Min / 3 Min (Optional)** | Precision Fine-Tuning | Only for refining entry once a 5m/15m setup is already confirmed (never trade 1m in isolation) |

### The "No-Man's Land" Rule
If price is halfway between major Support and major Resistance (e.g., 80 points from support and 80 points from resistance), **do not trade**. Reward-to-risk is poor and chop is highest in the middle of a range. Wait for price to reach a zone.
